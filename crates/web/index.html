Match day

You are on the bench, not watching a replay.

The match waits for you. Change what is not working while there is still time to change it.

  • Call timeouts to stop a run before it becomes a rout
  • Shout from the touchline: lift them, calm them, demand more
  • Make substitutions and switch offense, defense and press mid-game
  • Watch in 3D, from above, or as commentary only
  • A film room afterwards: your best attacks and what went wrong
Tactics

Draw it on the board. They run it.

Pick a system, then make it yours: drag your five to where you want them and draw the runs.

  • Five offenses, five defenses, three presses, each learned in training
  • A half-court board where the spots and cuts you draw are what the team plays
  • Tell each player what to do with the ball
  • Use a player out of position and he learns it, week by week
Squad

Twelve people, not twelve rows.

Every player has a body, a character and a career, and you can see all three.

  • Figures built from each player's height, reach, shoulders and weight
  • Morale, match sharpness, injuries and a medical record
  • Scouting that turns a guess about another club's player into knowledge
  • Fame that outlasts ability: an old star still fills the hall
Front office

Every club has someone to deal with.

The league runs on a salary cap, trades and a draft, and the club has to pay its way.

  • Trade players, picks and cash; each general manager values them his own way
  • Gate receipts, sponsors, ticket prices, board budgets and a luxury tax
  • Your own arena to fill, renovate and sell boards in
  • Coaches, scouts and a press officer to hire; an inbox that brings it all to you

The season starts when you do.

Pick a club, read the board's welcome, and play your first game.

Early version. Your career is saved automatically in this browser after each round; a match in progress is not, and a save may not survive a game update. Starting a new career replaces the saved one. There is one league, and no sound.
